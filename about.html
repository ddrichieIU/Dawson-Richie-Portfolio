I’m Dawson Richie, an Indianapolis-based filmmaker focused on video production. I work across directing, cinematography, lighting, and editing, taking a hands-on approach to the images and stories I create.

My portfolio includes independently produced work and collaborative crew productions. Each has given me a different perspective on how a film comes together, from shaping a scene on set to refining its pacing in post.
