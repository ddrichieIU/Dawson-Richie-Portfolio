About me.

I'm Dawson Richie, a filmmaker based in Indianapolis, Indiana.

My experience includes directing, cinematography, lighting, and editing. I enjoy being involved in different parts of the production process, both on set and in post-production.

I've worked on independently produced short films as well as collaborative productions with larger crews. Those experiences have given me opportunities to take on different responsibilities and learn how productions come together.

I'm continuing to develop my skills through new projects and opportunities to work with other filmmakers.
